# Testimonials — research moodboard (2025–2026)

Inspiration only. No layout, type, or motion is copied.

| Source | What was actually looked at |
| --- | --- |

## Named trends

1. **Pull quote as type** — hanging mark, rule, attribution.
2. **Photograph plate** — portrait is the object; quote is the caption.
3. **Deck / stack** — top card speaks; the pile is the control.
4. **Ledger of voices** — numbered entries, no card chrome.
5. **Cast index** — portraits drive a crossfade.
6. **Pinboard** — CSS columns, mixed weights, mask-to-more.
7. **Film strip** — next frame peeks; visitor drags.
8. **Held quote** — one card opens into a plate.
9. **Shallow 3D wall** — one shared key light.
10. **Physics reel** — cards have mass.
11. **Voice object** — waveform + transcript. No autoplay.

## Saturated — do not ship

Infinite logo+quote marquees. Five gold stars as chrome. Fake Jack/@jack. Glass swipers. Quote spheres. Helio default voices.
