Inspiration only. No layout, type, or motion is copied.

## Named trends

1. **Ranked mosaic** — one cell is twice the others. The size *is* the sentence.
2. **Working instruments** — sparkline, filter, compare live inside a cell.
3. **Evidence crops** — the picture is the tile; caption is a caption.
4. **Index → plate** — a list drives one shared surface.
5. **Expand-in-place** — selected cell becomes the detail without a modal.
6. **Paper compartments** — rules, not cards. Editorial counter-aesthetic.
7. **Pointer reweight** — neighbours yield when one cell is focused.
8. **Named desk objects** — tiles do not share a radius.
9. **Scrub inside a cell** — control-first media, no page hijack.
10. **Equal reel** — when features are honestly equal, do not fake a hero cell.

## Saturated — do not ship

2+4+2 icon cards. Glow borders. Six identical title+paragraph cells at different sizes. Helio night-desk objects as defaults. Skeleton app chrome. Magnify-as-the-idea.

## Library-useful

Ranked mosaic. Paper sheet. Evidence well. Working dock. Shared stage. Spec figures. Expand. Inspect. Desk of objects. Reflow.
