## Named trends

1. **Scroll-as-transition** — media expands or morphs on native scroll. 21st expansion hero; Apple product pages.
2. **Contained instrument** — WebGL as a named object in a plate. Hut 8; Bruno Simon.
3. **Print-fold chrome** — rails, hatch, L-corners, dither inset. Aceternity framed/dither heroes.
4. **Prompt as primary control** — a real field, not two pills. Aceternity chat-input hero.
5. **Live workspace** — a working window as evidence. Aceternity ticker+workspace.
6. **Kinetic variable type** — letters have mass. Obys; FORMAT.
7. **Cream editorial counter** — paper, serif, one clay accent. Anthropic.
8. **Image fan / collage** — overlapping photographs. 21st image-fan; Pinterest boards.
9. **Hub-and-spoke diagram** — SVG beams as product proof. Aceternity hub.
10. **Assetless horizon** — CSS/canvas depth, no mp4. 21st Shape Landing / Horizon.

## Saturated — do not ship

Centered sparkle stack. Mesh/aurora/fluid as the idea. Hero Parallax row farms. Beams+grid “AI atmosphere.” Full-bleed shader wallpaper. Autoplay video. MacBook-lid scroll. Word-rotate as the whole hero. Logo marquee without a product. Dark navy + violet mesh. Industry costumes (SaaS window, two phones, `$48/mo`).

## Library-useful (finite props, reduced-motion end state)

Print-fold / editorial slab. Prompt-first field. Image fan. Live product frame. Native scroll expansion (no hijack). Contained WebGL with a required still. Kinetic type with a way through. Flip-board as an object (not a countdown kit).
