## Named trends

1. **Period control as object** — the saving lives on the switch.
2. **Seat / usage sliders** — the number is the buyer’s, not the marketer’s.
3. **Difference tables** — rows that diverge, not 40 identical checks.
4. **One published figure** — single offer, two actions.
5. **Opposed rooms** — self-serve vs sales, personal vs team.
6. **Ruled ledger** — boxless columns, hairlines align.
7. **Working quote** — formula → breakdown → total.
8. **Morph** — cards and table are one layout that transforms.
9. **Dossier** — chapters, filter, sticky summary.
10. **Cumulative radios** — selection is a ring, not a neon badge.

## Saturated — do not ship

Three `$99` cards with checkmarks. Neon Recommended. Baked prices as defaults. Enterprise as fog. Dual-axis as a twelfth id.
