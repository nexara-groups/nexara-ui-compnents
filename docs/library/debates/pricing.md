# Pricing — role debate

Six agents argued before implementation. They did not agree on names. They agreed on the kills.

## Roles

- Trend researcher
- Product / UX strategist
- Visual designer
- Motion designer
- Front-end (API / perf / a11y)
- Sceptical creative director

## Fights

**Three cards vs a decision.** Researcher: 2026 pages still ship a ladder. CD: if the family is three cards with a glow, it is a costume rack. UX: most pastes *are* three plans. Converged: `slab` is the honest ladder. `offer` and `pair` exist when the decision is not three. Glow dies.

**Calculator as theater.** Motion wanted a cinematic quote. FE: the slider must end on a number, labelled, keyboardable. UX: burying the calculator under a table is why usage pages fail. Converged: `quote` is advanced and working. `seats` and `meter` are intermediate objects.

**Table is a section.** Visual wanted the matrix to be the page. UX agreed when features are the product. FE: real `<table>`, row notes, no div soup. Converged: `table` is intermediate. `morph` transforms slab ↔ table.

**Defaults.** CD killed `$48/mo`, Helio Studio, checkmark poems. Empty plans omit cards. Amounts are props.

**What died.** Neon Recommended. A 12th add-on marketplace. Dual-axis as an id (it is a slot).
