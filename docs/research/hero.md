# hero

Surveyed 21st.dev Aceternity (Hero, Hero Parallax, Hero Highlight, Lamp) and community heroes (shader, video, arc gallery). Editorial, cinematic, framed, poster, band, and rail change the layout.

Sources: [21st.dev](https://21st.dev), [Aceternity on 21st](https://21st.dev/@manuarora700/library/aceternity-ui), [Motion Primitives](https://docs.21st.dev/@ibelick/library/motion-primitives), [Spectrum UI](https://docs.21st.dev/@arihantcodes_1f7b8c4d/library/spectrum-ui), plus public catalogues from shadcn/ui, HeroUI, Magic UI, Park UI, and Uiverse. Ideas were reimplemented. No source was copied.

## Selected

- `center` (Center): Stacked, centered intro.
- `split` (Split): Copy beside a media slot.
- `editorial` (Editorial): Huge serif title and a rule before the eyebrow.
- `cinematic` (Cinematic): Copy sits low while media fills the back.
- `poster` (Poster): Uppercase title, narrow measure.
- `framed` (Framed): Media sits in a window chrome.
- `overlap` (Overlap): Copy and media share the same band.
- `quiet` (Quiet): Sans title, more air.
- `band` (Band): Actions sit in a full-width strip.
- `asymmetric` (Asymmetric): Wide copy column, narrow media.
- `stacked` (Stacked): Media first, then the title.
- `rail` (Rail): Eyebrow runs down the side.

## Rejected

- Extra colour swaps of an existing treatment.
- Variants that only changed duration or opacity.

## Round 2

Round 2 removed editorial, poster, framed, and quiet. Added mast, statement, bento, and index. Overlap is a 12-column overlap. Rail puts the eyebrow in its own cell. Kept center, split, cinematic, band, asymmetric, and stacked.
