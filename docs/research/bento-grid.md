# bento-grid

Surveyed 21st.dev Aceternity (Bento Grid, Layout Grid) and Magic UI bento blocks, plus editorial, terminal, poster, and brutal tile ideas that those galleries do not cover as separate tiles.

Sources: [21st.dev](https://21st.dev), [Aceternity on 21st](https://21st.dev/@manuarora700/library/aceternity-ui), [Motion Primitives](https://docs.21st.dev/@ibelick/library/motion-primitives), [Spectrum UI](https://docs.21st.dev/@arihantcodes_1f7b8c4d/library/spectrum-ui), plus public catalogues from shadcn/ui, HeroUI, Magic UI, Park UI, and Uiverse. Ideas were reimplemented. No source was copied.

## Selected

- `spotlight` (Spotlight): Pointer wash on a dark tile.
- `glass` (Glass): Frosted tile.
- `outline` (Outline): Border only.
- `editorial` (Editorial): Open tile closed by a baseline rule.
- `terminal` (Terminal): Monospace tile with a tide border.
- `ticket` (Ticket): Dashed ticket edge.
- `poster` (Poster): Flare field and heavy type.
- `metric` (Metric): Oversized figure, small caption.
- `neon` (Neon): Iris edge with a bloom.
- `inset` (Inset): Recessed tile.
- `soft` (Soft): Large radius, no hard border.
- `brutal` (Brutal): Offset paper shadow.

## Rejected

- Extra colour swaps of an existing treatment.
- Variants that only changed duration or opacity.

## Round 2

Round 2 replaced glass with chart (sparkline), outline with quote, soft with media (full-bleed plus scrim), inset with compare (before/after), neon with status (pulse), ticket with schedule (time gutter), and metric with stat. Poster uses a dark scrim and paper type. Kept editorial, terminal, brutal, and spotlight.
