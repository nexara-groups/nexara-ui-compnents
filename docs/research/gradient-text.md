# gradient-text

Surveyed 21st.dev Aceternity (Colourful Text, Text Hover, Flip Words, Encrypted Text, Sparkles) and Motion Primitives (Text Shimmer, Text Shimmer Wave, Text Scramble). Stroke, marker, glitch, wave, and kinetic tracking are the non-colour treatments.

Sources: [21st.dev](https://21st.dev), [Aceternity on 21st](https://21st.dev/@manuarora700/library/aceternity-ui), [Motion Primitives](https://docs.21st.dev/@ibelick/library/motion-primitives), [Spectrum UI](https://docs.21st.dev/@arihantcodes_1f7b8c4d/library/spectrum-ui), plus public catalogues from shadcn/ui, HeroUI, Magic UI, Park UI, and Uiverse. Ideas were reimplemented. No source was copied.

## Selected

- `sweep` (Sweep): Gradient pans across the glyphs.
- `static` (Static): The same blend, held still.
- `shimmer` (Shimmer): A bright band runs through the word.
- `stroke` (Stroke): Outline only, no fill.
- `marker` (Marker): A highlighter sits under the words.
- `neon` (Neon): Gradient fill plus a colored glow.
- `glitch` (Glitch): Offset color copies jitter behind the word.
- `wave` (Wave): Letters bob on a delay.
- `clip` (Clip): Hard color blocks, no soft blend.
- `kinetic` (Kinetic): Tracking breathes in and out.
- `underline` (Underline): Solid type with a moving gradient rule.
- `aurora` (Aurora): Four-stop field that keeps moving.

## Rejected

- Extra colour swaps of an existing treatment.
- Variants that only changed duration or opacity.

## Round 2

Round 2 replaced static, neon, and aurora with flicker, spark, and flip. Kept sweep, shimmer, stroke, marker, glitch, wave, clip, kinetic, and underline.
