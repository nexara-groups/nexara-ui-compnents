# draggable-card

Surveyed 21st.dev Aceternity (Draggable Card, Comet Card, Card Stack, Glare Card), Spectrum UI (Login Card, Wallet-style cards via SmoothUI), and Uiverse card treatments. Colour-only swaps were dropped.

Sources: [21st.dev](https://21st.dev), [Aceternity on 21st](https://21st.dev/@manuarora700/library/aceternity-ui), [Motion Primitives](https://docs.21st.dev/@ibelick/library/motion-primitives), [Spectrum UI](https://docs.21st.dev/@arihantcodes_1f7b8c4d/library/spectrum-ui), plus public catalogues from shadcn/ui, HeroUI, Magic UI, Park UI, and Uiverse. Ideas were reimplemented. No source was copied.

## Selected

- `glass` (Glass): Frosted panel that stays readable over a busy stage.
- `solid` (Solid): Opaque ink card with a quiet shadow.
- `outline` (Outline): Hairline edge and a nearly clear face.
- `elevated` (Elevated): Lifted gradient face with a colored shadow.
- `ticket` (Ticket): Dashed perforation down the leading edge.
- `polaroid` (Polaroid): Paper print with a thick caption band.
- `dossier` (Dossier): Ruled file card with a corner stamp.
- `neon-slab` (Neon slab): Dark slab with a glowing iris edge.
- `folded` (Folded): Dog-ear in the corner, as if the sheet was turned.
- `receipt` (Receipt): Monospace slip with a torn bottom edge.
- `stack` (Stack): Offset sheets sit behind the card you drag.
- `comic` (Comic): Ink border, halftone, and a hard flare shadow.

## Rejected

- Extra colour swaps of an existing treatment.
- Variants that only changed duration or opacity.

## Round 2

Round 2 replaced solid with film (sprockets), outline with sticker (tape and tilt), elevated with window (drag from the title bar), neon-slab with snap-grid (release snaps to 24px), and folded with carbon (a dashed sheet lags the drag). Kept glass, ticket, polaroid, dossier, receipt, stack, and comic.
