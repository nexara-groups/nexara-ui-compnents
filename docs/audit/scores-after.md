## Still not at the bar

1. **Primitive chrome families.** Button soft/ghost, input outline/filled/underline, badge solid/soft/outline, toast statuses. These match Origin / shadcn. A lead who wants twelve unique objects will still call them skins.
2. **Aurora fields.** Several dark washes differ by hue more than structure.
3. **Illustrated media.** Shop lamp, app phones, weather sun, place map are stands-ins. Denser than the old empty panes. Not production screenshots.
4. **Magnetic capsules.** Pill, glow, and glass still read as one control.

## What cleared the system reject

- Light and dark both render.
- Geist / Instrument Serif, quiet accent.
- Modal and tooltip galleries show the object at rest.
- Heroes no longer share one title.
- No wax, tickets, hazard tape, lightning glyphs, or rivets.
