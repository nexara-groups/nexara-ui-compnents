# Text Reveal

## Round 1
Words traveled through the line above them. Character mode could not wrap. The first paint was invisible, and the motion was a one-shot fade rather than a scroll reveal.

## Round 2
Each unit is clipped, the full string is in one screen-reader node, and opacity follows scroll progress. The demo spacer was too short, so the line opened already mostly lit.

## Round 3
The demo spacer is a full frame, so the line starts below the fold.

## Agreement
Approved. The line brightens as it is scrolled up through the frame.

## Variants
rise, blur, uncover, fade, skew, rotate, highlight, tracking, slide, wipe, pop, drop.

Uncover rises inside a clip, drop enters from above, wipe opens across the line, and skew unskews. These replaced clip, scale, and mask.

## Agreement
Approved with the text set.

## Structural pass
Rise, blur, uncover, skew, wipe, and pop were one line and a different entrance. Caption, stat, stack, and kicker were the same line with a label, a size, or a position change. They became a registered figure, a medallion, two columns, and a running head. Final set: editorial, headline, marker, outline, split, figure, quote, medallion, columns, annotation, index, running-head. The strict critic approved them. Builder agrees.
