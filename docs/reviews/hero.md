# Hero

## Round 1
The heading level was fixed, centered media stretched full width, and the entrance finished on mount even when the section was below the fold.

## Round 2
`titleAs` chooses the heading. The section is named from that heading. Centered media is capped. The stagger runs when the block enters view, and the demo remounts it when the layout changes.

## Agreement
Approved. The section is a named intro whose entrance waits until it is on screen.

## Variants
center, split, mast, cinematic, statement, bento, overlap, index, band, asymmetric, stacked, rail.

Overlap shares columns 6–7. Rail gives the eyebrow its own cell. Mast is a nameplate, a full-width title, and a rule between the lede and the actions. Cinematic keeps the media inside the section.

## Agreement
Approved.

## Structural pass
Center, split, mast, cinematic, statement, overlap, and rail were one headline block with a different column arrangement. They were replaced by twelve pages: saas, download, video, logos, pricing, agency, commerce, event, search, dashboard, editorial, waitlist. The strict critic approved them. Builder agrees.
