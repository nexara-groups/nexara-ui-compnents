# Round 2 — interaction / motion

**Lens:** motion with a job, reduced-motion paths, pointer vs keyboard.  
**Evidence:** live docs plus after galleries.

## Verdict

**Approve the motion contract.** Buttons scale on press. Magnetic still pulls only on a fine pointer. Typewriter, text-reveal, aurora, and marquee honor `prefers-reduced-motion`. Modal trap and scroll lock still run on the live dialog, not on `inline` gallery panels.

**Hold:** beam and shimmer are still ornament. They are current (Magic UI language) but they are not information. I keep them because the owner asked for that shelf, not because they teach the control.

Draggable tilt and flip Y-turn are the same mechanics as round 1. The objects changed; the springs did not get worse.

## Debate

A11y asked whether twelve open inline modals lock the body. They do not. `inline` skips the trap. Good.

Visual asked to kill shimmer. I voted keep: it is the one CTA that is not a flat fill, and reduced motion freezes it.
