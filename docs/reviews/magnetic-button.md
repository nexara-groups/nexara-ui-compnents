# Magnetic Button

## Round 1
The pull used an angle from the center, so the control lunged sideways at the midpoint and went slack at the rim. Proximity was layout padding, and the icon did not travel with the label.

## Round 2
Movement is the clamped offset from center, proximity is cancelled with a negative margin, and icons sit in the shifting label. The demo’s strength still reached the neighboring control.

## Round 3
The demo uses a wider gap, and each control’s travel stays inside its own hit pad.

## Agreement
Approved. The buttons lean toward the pointer without covering each other.

## Variants
attract, tilt, repel, stretch, sling, snap, orbit, lift, glow, wobble, peel, press.

Round 1 asked to replace lag and chase. Sling flings on the exit velocity and springs home. Peel shears the label. Glow keeps the shell still and trails a slower copy of the label. Wobble rotates from pointer speed. Tilt leans on X and Y. The proximity padding no longer expands the click target.

## Agreement
Approved with the button set.

## Structural pass
The spring set (attract, tilt, repel, stretch, sling, snap, orbit, lift, glow, wobble, peel, press) was one pill. It was replaced by different objects. The strict critic then rejected chip (a recolored pill) and tube (a flat label that was not a neon tube). Those became a perforated stamp and a notched price tag. Final set: pill, keycap, bulb, dock, sticker, medal, stamp, badge, shutter, ticket, tag, wax. Critic approved. Builder agrees.
