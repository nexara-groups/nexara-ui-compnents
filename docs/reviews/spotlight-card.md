# Spotlight Card

## Round 1
The documented diameter was used as a gradient radius, so the light filled the card. The first hover faded in at a fallback point, and the hairline color was fixed.

## Round 2
The spot size is a real diameter, coordinates are set on pointer enter, and the hairline color and hover lift are props.

## Agreement
Approved. The follow reads as a spotlight rather than a full-card wash.
