# Flip Card

## Round 1
Click mode had no focus ring and a generic name. Both faces were exposed to assistive tech. Hover mode hid a focused link on mouse leave.

## Round 2
The hidden face is inert. A Show back / Show front control is the keyboard path and has a visible focus ring. Mouse leave does not hide the card while focus remains inside.

## Agreement
Approved. The turn is a real perspective flip, and the control is keyboard-reachable.

## Variants
yaw, pitch, hinge-left, shuffle, fade, slide, split, iris, peel, box, shutter, fold.

The face that is leaving is inert. Fold hinges on the bottom edge. Box carries a side wall. Iris and shutter are wipes. Peel pulls the front from the corner.

## Agreement
Approved.

## Structural pass
Yaw, pitch, hinge, fade, slide, iris, peel, and fold were one card and a different turn. They were replaced by twelve objects that share one Y-turn: credit, badge, playing, product, flashcard, ticket, profile, pricing, passport, vinyl, book, postcard. The strict critic approved them. Builder agrees.
