## Verdict

**REJECT**

Does not reach the live sites. Does not exceed them. I would not put this in front of a client who already ships Sri, Rise, Nexara, or VDRSA.

The inventory grew. Agents can now ask for `hero / ac-remote`, `kit / healthcare`, `stats / countup` and get the right *page model*. That is cataloguing, not a visual win over production photography.

## What I will sign

- The map: 30 hero names, 12-variant section blocks, five industry kits, Agency OS chrome.
- Owner-world copy (Hyderabad, EECP, VRF, Sai Nirmaan, VDRSA).
- The AC remote *behavior* (power, 16–30°, mode, fan).
- `button / group`, `button / toggle`, `data-table / filter` as skeletons.

## What I will not pretend

- That an SVG split unit is Sri’s photographic plant.
- That a pink heart mark is Rise’s anatomical still.
- That typeset NEXARA on CSS rings is the live metallic wordmark.
- That `hero / sports` is a full-bleed photo.
- That 30 named heroes compete with 21st.dev’s scenes. Empty fans, conic donuts, and two blur orbs are layout names.

**Brief check:** reach *and* exceed the live sites. Still short of reach. Exceed is not on the table.

## Consensus

Visual and lead reject the ship-as-equal claim. Motion and a11y give a conditional on the *system* after the pause/contrast follow-up. Nobody approves the library as exceeding the owner sites.
