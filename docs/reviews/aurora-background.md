# Aurora Background

## Round 1
Solid discs shared one nudge, the grain tiled coarsely, and pause snapped the fields back to their start.

## Round 2
Fields are radial gradients on opposing paths. Pause uses `animation-play-state`. A scrim sits under the type, and the grain is quieter.

## Agreement
Approved. The demo reads as a dark aurora behind the headline.

## Variants
drift, beams, stars, waves, ripple, vortex, meteors, embers, spotlight, horizon, ribbons, scan.

Beams keep their angle while they drift, because the rotation is the CSS rotate property rather than a transform the keyframes overwrite. Stars use the palette.

## Agreement
Approved.

## Structural pass
Drift, waves, ripple, vortex, spotlight, ribbons, and scan were the same field with a different motion. Beams, embers, caustics, and nebula repeated curtains, particles, or the mesh. They became radar, clouds, mountains, and lightning. Final set: aurora, starfield, meteors, mesh, radar, sunset, clouds, rain, constellation, mountains, city, lightning. The strict critic approved them. Builder agrees.
